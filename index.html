Rakesh Kumar Sahoo

I am a PhD Scholar in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Bhubaneswar, working on the numerical solution of nonlinear partial differential equations under the supervision of Dr. Bankim Chandra Mandal.

My research interests include Domain Decomposition Methods, Scientific Computing, Numerical Analysis, and Partial Differential Equations. This website presents my research, academic background, publications, talks, teaching activities, and future work.

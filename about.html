About Me

A brief introduction to my academic background and research.

Rakesh Kumar Sahoo

I am a PhD Scholar in the Department of Mathematics, IIT Bhubaneswar.

My doctoral research focuses on the numerical solution of nonlinear partial differential equations. Specifically, I am interested in developing reliable and efficient domain decomposition techniques to solve these challenging mathematical problems.

My research is being carried out under the supervision of Dr. Bankim Chandra Mandal.
